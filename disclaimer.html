Last updated: June 17, 2026

The Book describes one maker's methods, opinions, and preferences. It is not professional advice and is not a substitute for the safety data sheets (SDS), warning labels, and instructions supplied with your wax, fragrance, wicks, dyes, equipment, and containers. Where the Book and a manufacturer's instructions disagree, follow the manufacturer.

2. Fire, heat & hot-wax safety

  • Never leave melting wax or a burning candle unattended.
  • Melt wax gently using a double-boiler method; never melt wax directly over an open flame or in a way that can exceed its flash point.
  • Keep a suitable fire extinguisher nearby. Never use water on a wax or grease fire.
  • Work in a ventilated area, away from drafts, flammable materials, children, and pets.
  • Hot wax causes burns. Use heat-resistant gloves, protect surfaces, and let equipment cool fully before handling.
  • Always test-burn finished candles in a safe, controlled setting before use or gifting.

4. Fragrance, IFRA & regulations

Fragrance load percentages, flash points, and IFRA guidance in the Book are general starting points. Always verify the current IFRA documentation and usage rates for your specific fragrance from your supplier, and comply with all laws and regulations that apply to you.
