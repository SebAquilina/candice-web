Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Template notice. This is a general privacy policy for a small digital-product site. Adjust the named third-party services to match what you actually use, and have it reviewed for GDPR/CCPA compliance if you sell into those regions.

1. What we collect

  • Order information you provide at checkout (name, email, and payment details handled by our payment processor — we never see or store full card numbers).
  • Email address if you opt in to updates, new recipes, or the newsletter.
  • Usage data such as pages visited and device/browser type, collected through cookies and basic analytics to improve the site.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver the Book and bonuses and provide support.
  • To process payments and handle refunds.
  • To send updates and offers only if you opted in (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To understand and improve how the site performs.

3. Third parties we rely on

We use trusted third-party services to run the business — for example a payment processor and digital- delivery platform (such as Stripe, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, or Payhip), an email provider, and website analytics. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies for essential site function and analytics. You can control cookies in your browser settings; disabling some may affect how the site works.

5. Your choices & rights

  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time via the link in any message.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your personal data by emailing us.
  • Residents of certain regions (e.g., the EU/UK and California) may have additional rights under GDPR/CCPA; we honor valid requests.

6. Data retention & security

We keep order records as long as needed for tax, legal, and support purposes, and take reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children

This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Privacy questions or requests? Email hello@the3dollarcandle.com.
